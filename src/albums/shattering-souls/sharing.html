Shatter the silence

All songs. No vocals. Add yours!

These songs were born out of a need to process real, heavy, life-altering experiences through pure sonic force. But pain and survival aren't solitary journeys. If these riffs speak to your own story, I invite you to step up to the microphone. I’m stripping away my vocals and providing the raw, instrumental tracks for anyone who needs a heavy canvas to scream, sing, or speak their own truth. Use them for covers, use them for practice, or use them as a sonic exorcism for whatever you're fighting through. Take the tracks. Add your voice. Shatter the walls.
